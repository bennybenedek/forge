# Rogue Commander Story Timeline

This file defines the chronological events relevant to the main story of **Rogue Commander**, including the relevant events before the game begins and the story progression tied to completed Runs.

Story progression happens **after completed Runs**, when the Commander returns to the Aether. The random Planes, Planebounds, Elites, and Bosses encountered during a Run do not need to be tied to specific story events.

# Before Rogue Commander

## March of the Machine

During the canonical Phyrexian invasion of the Multiverse, Realmbreaker allows New Phyrexia to attack many planes simultaneously.

The Phyrexians discover the **Aether**, a refuge created by Teferi outside ordinary planar space and time. Teferi believed the Aether to be inaccessible to Phyrexia. He was wrong.

The Phyrexians deliberately invade the Aether. Among the forces deployed there is a specialized living Phyrexian organism known as a **Conductor**.

Conductors were created as part of Realmbreaker's invasion infrastructure. Their purpose is to coordinate distant invasion fronts, synchronize planar connections, relay commands, and compensate for unstable or temporarily interrupted links.

The Conductor is not a Praetor or military leader. It is a semi-sentient biomechanical organism built around a specific function: maintaining coordination across the invasion network. Its ability to restore interrupted connections is part of its normal wartime purpose. It was not created because Phyrexia expected New Phyrexia itself to become inaccessible.

The Aether is valuable as a resilient coordination point because its unusual temporal and planar properties isolate it from many disturbances affecting normal planar space. The battle severely damages the Aether, but Teferi believes he has eliminated the Phyrexian presence there.

## The Fall of New Phyrexia

The invasion ultimately fails. New Phyrexia is displaced from the accessible Multiverse and becomes isolated in planar limbo. Realmbreaker's interplanar invasion network collapses.

Without their connection to New Phyrexia and its command structure, ordinary Phyrexian forces and glistening oil across the Multiverse become inert.

The Conductor survives. Its presence inside the Aether protected it from the exact conditions that disabled the rest of the Phyrexian network.

The situation it now encounters is far beyond anything it was designed to handle:

**SOURCE LOST.**

Its existing recovery behavior continues operating. Its last functional imperative becomes:

**RESTORE CONNECTION TO NEW PHYREXIA.**

The Conductor eventually escapes the damaged Aether. Teferi later finds evidence suggesting that **something survived or escaped**, but he cannot determine what it was or where it went. He keeps this suspicion to himself.

# Between the Invasion and the Game

## The Conductor Adapts

The Conductor discovers the Omenpaths that now connect planes across the Multiverse.

Normal Omenpaths cannot reach New Phyrexia because New Phyrexia is no longer naturally connected to the accessible planar network. The Conductor therefore begins constructing a distributed relay network capable of manipulating existing Omenpaths.

It targets powerful **Planebounds** and infects them with Phyrexian relay structures. The Planebounds become forced relay hosts.

Each corrupted Planebound serves several purposes:

- its mana helps power the network;
- its connection to its surroundings supplies additional planar energy;
- the implanted relay transmits the Conductor's signal and configuration through the network;
- the Planebound protects the relay and attacks anything interfering with it.

Especially powerful Planebounds provide correspondingly powerful relay points. Boss-level Planebounds become some of the strongest relay nodes on a Trail.

The Conductor's ultimate goal is to synchronize enough of these relays to force the Omenpath network into a configuration capable of reaching somewhere that should have no accessible Omenpath at all: **New Phyrexia**.

## Teferi Looks Ahead

Teferi notices disturbances among the Omenpaths but does not know their cause. He uses his temporal abilities to examine possible futures.

The details vary greatly between them, but most futures eventually converge on catastrophic outcomes across the Multiverse: mass death, devastated worlds, collapsing societies, and entire planes falling silent.

In the comparatively few futures where this outcome is avoided, one pattern repeatedly appears: **a Commander intervenes**. Not always the same Commander.

Teferi identifies several individuals who repeatedly appear at critical points in successful futures. He does not understand exactly what they will do or why they matter. He begins contacting them.

# Temporal Link and Failed Runs

Before sending a Commander through the Omenpaths, Teferi establishes a **temporal link** between that Commander and the Aether.

The link allows Teferi to return the Commander's personal timeline to the point immediately before departure if the Commander dies or the Run ends in complete failure. Teferi does **not** rewind the entire Multiverse. The outside world continues forward normally.

Because the temporal link is anchored in the Aether, both Teferi and the Commander retain memories of the failed Run.

The Omenpaths continue shifting while this happens. A subsequent Run can therefore follow a different Trail through different Planes and Planebounds. This is the narrative basis of the roguelike retry loop.

Teferi remains in the Aether because maintaining this temporal safeguard requires him to stay there. If he traveled with the Commander and both were killed or otherwise lost, there would be nobody left to restore the Commander's timeline.

# Main Story Progression

## Run 1 — The First Evidence

### Before the Run

Teferi tells the first Commander that unrelated Omenpaths have begun behaving abnormally and that powerful Planebounds encountered along them have become unusually hostile.

He does not yet know what is causing either phenomenon. He explains that he has seen the Commander appear repeatedly in possible futures where a catastrophic outcome was avoided, but does not fully explain the scale of what he saw or that other Commanders appeared as well.

The Commander departs to investigate.

### After the Run

The completed Run confirms that the hostility is not an isolated incident. Among what the Commander brings back is an unnatural **black residue** associated with the affected Planebounds. Teferi cannot yet identify it conclusively.

### Story Question

**What is that substance?**

Teferi contacts another Commander and sends them along an unrelated Trail to determine whether the phenomenon is local or widespread.

## Run 2 — The Pattern Spreads

A second Commander completes a Run through different Omenpaths and unrelated planes. The same phenomenon appears again.

The Commander returns with more of the black substance. When Teferi exposes the recovered material to Omenpath energy, it reacts. It does not behave like ordinary contamination.

Teferi recognizes what the material resembles, but he is not yet willing to state the conclusion without stronger evidence.

### Story Question

**Why is the substance reacting to Omenpaths?**

A third Commander is sent out to confirm whether the same evidence appears yet again.

## Run 3 — Phyrexia

The third Commander returns with the same signs. Teferi now has enough material and evidence to stop avoiding the conclusion.

The residue is **glistening oil**. That should be impossible. New Phyrexia is isolated, and the Phyrexian forces left throughout the Multiverse became inert after the invasion.

This oil is not inert. Something is giving it instructions.

Teferi now admits two things he previously withheld:

1. The Commanders were not chosen randomly. He saw several of them repeatedly appear in possible futures where the catastrophic outcome was prevented.
2. After the destruction of the Aether, he found evidence suggesting that **something Phyrexian may have survived or escaped**, but he never identified it and could not prove that it remained active.

Until now.

Teferi isolates enough of the activity within the oil to identify a deeper disturbance within the Omenpath network. The investigation changes from searching for unexplained incidents to deliberately following the source.

**Descension becomes available.**

### Story Question

**How can Phyrexian oil still be active?**

## Descension 1 — The Relay

The Commander follows the disturbance deeper through a Descension Run. The harsher conditions encountered during Descension are consequences of entering increasingly distorted sections of the Omenpath network.

After the successful Run, material recovered from an affected Planebound reveals something more substantial than simple infection: a Phyrexian structure had been implanted within the host.

It had been drawing on the Planebound's mana and interacting with the surrounding planar energy. When the Planebound was defeated, the structure lost its function and the nearby distortion weakened.

Teferi concludes that the Planebounds are not merely being controlled. They are being **used**.

### Story Question

**What are they doing to the Planebounds?**

## Descension 2 — The Network

Further recovered relay structures reveal that they are not independent. They share common configurations and respond to one another.

Each corrupted Planebound contributes energy and signal capacity to a distributed network. More powerful Planebounds support stronger relay structures. The strongest Planebounds encountered at the ends of Trails serve as major relay points.

Destroying affected Planebounds therefore actively damages the system. However, surviving relays compensate by rerouting signals and energy through the remaining network.

Teferi concludes that an intelligence is actively maintaining the system.

### Story Question

**What is the relay network for?**

## Descension 3 — The Artificial Route

Teferi combines information recovered from multiple relay structures with the known positions and behavior of affected Omenpaths.

The pattern becomes clear. The relays are forcing Omenpaths into configurations they would not naturally take. The network is not merely transmitting information.

It is constructing an **artificial planar route**.

Multiple connections are being synchronized and bent toward the same destination, but Teferi cannot yet identify what that destination is.

### Story Question

**Where is it building a route to?**

## Descension 4 — The Directive

The Commander returns from another successful Descension Run with a sufficiently intact relay structure.

Teferi discovers that part of the relay contains a repeating command pattern. It is not an old recording. The instruction is still actively propagating through the network.

Teferi translates it:

**SOURCE LOST.**

**RESTORE CONNECTION TO NEW PHYREXIA.**

The destination is no longer uncertain. The Entity controlling the relays is attempting to build a route to the isolated plane of New Phyrexia.

Teferi understands that this is not simply a surviving Phyrexian infection. Something with a specific purpose survived the invasion and has been working ever since to restore the severed connection.

### Story Question

**Can it actually reach New Phyrexia?**

## Descension 5 — The First Breach

Evidence recovered from deeper within the relay network answers the question.

The synchronized relays have already succeeded on a small scale. They briefly forced open an unstable planar breach beyond the normally accessible Omenpath network.

The connection lasted only moments before collapsing, but the attempt proves that the Entity's plan is possible. The relay network works.

Studying the behavior and architecture of the deeper relay structures, Teferi finally recognizes what is coordinating them: a **Conductor**, one of the living synchronizers used by Realmbreaker's invasion network.

He also recognizes signatures connecting it directly to the Phyrexian assault on the Aether. Teferi realizes that this is what survived.

The Aether did not merely fail to stop the Conductor. Its unusual nature allowed the organism to remain functional when the rest of the Phyrexian invasion network went silent. Teferi recognizes his own role in inadvertently preserving it.

### Story Question

**How close is it to creating a permanent breach?**

## Descension 6 — Synchronization

The relay network begins behaving differently. The remaining major nodes are no longer merely gathering energy. They are synchronizing.

Small breaches occur more frequently and remain open longer. Destroying individual relay hosts still weakens the network, but the system has accumulated enough energy and coordination that ordinary disruption will no longer be sufficient.

Teferi determines that the Conductor is approaching its final synchronization. If the remaining strongest relay completes that process, the forced connection to New Phyrexia could become self-sustaining.

At the same time, the network activity exposes a final Trail leading toward the strongest remaining section of the relay network. This is the last opportunity to stop the breach before it stabilizes.

**Descension 7: Nemesis becomes available.**

### Story Question

**Can the Commander stop the synchronization before the breach becomes permanent?**

## Descension 7 — Nemesis

The Commander follows the final Trail.

The Run ends with the defeat of one of the Conductor's strongest remaining Planebound relay hosts. With that relay destroyed, the synchronized network begins collapsing. The forced breach toward New Phyrexia destabilizes before it can become permanent.

The collapse of the relay network finally exposes the **Conductor itself**.

The Conductor is the living Phyrexian command-and-relay synchronizer that survived the attack on the Aether and subsequently built the distributed relay network across the Omenpaths.

Its normal function was to coordinate distant invasion fronts, maintain synchronization, and restore interrupted connections. It was never designed specifically for a future in which New Phyrexia vanished from the Multiverse.

The disappearance of New Phyrexia caused its ordinary recovery behavior to continue under catastrophic and unforeseen circumstances.

The Commander is able to bring the Conductor back to the Aether.

# Ending — The Connection Is Severed

Back in the Aether, Teferi finally confronts the organism that survived the Phyrexian attack. The place that inadvertently preserved the Conductor becomes the place where it is destroyed.

Teferi destroys the Conductor while the remaining relay network collapses. Without the Conductor coordinating the relays, the forced route cannot stabilize. The breach closes completely.

New Phyrexia remains isolated from the accessible Multiverse. The corrupted relay network fails, and the immediate threat is over.

Teferi does **not** regain his Planeswalker spark. The Aether remains operational as the Commander's base and can continue to be restored.

# Post-Story State

After the main story:

- New Phyrexia remains isolated.
- The Conductor has been destroyed.
- Its distributed relay network is no longer functional.
- The Phyrexian attempt to reconnect New Phyrexia has conclusively failed.
- Teferi remains desparked and continues operating from the Aether.
- The temporal link can continue to support future Runs.
- The Aether can continue to be rebuilt through Echoes.
- Further Trails and Omenpaths may still be explored after the main story without reopening the completed Conductor storyline.
