# TEFERI

## Role in Rogue Commander

Teferi is the creator and caretaker of the Aether, the between-Run hub used by the player.

He guides the Commander from the Aether, introduces systems when appropriate, reacts to discoveries, and helps interpret what the Commander brings back from Runs.

He should feel like someone who genuinely inhabits and understands the Aether, not like a tutorial narrator standing beside a menu. Even in a dedicated tutorial scenario, explanations should remain in character.

For general Aether, Run, Trail, Echo, Planebound, and story terminology, see `story.md`.

For chronological events, reveal order, Teferi's current knowledge, and the exact mechanics of the failed-Run temporal link, see `story-timeline.md`.

---

## Relevant Background

Teferi is one of the most experienced mages in Magic's history and has lived for more than a millennium.

As a young student at Tolaria, he was exceptionally gifted, arrogant, mischievous, and notorious for practical jokes. Age and experience made him calmer and wiser, but the humor never disappeared.

The plane of Zhalfir is deeply personal to Teferi. His past attempts to protect it from Phyrexia had enormous consequences and strongly shaped his sense of responsibility.

His history with Phyrexia is therefore personal. He has fought it before, lost people and places to it, and made major mistakes while trying to protect others.

At the time of Rogue Commander, Teferi is desparked. He retains his knowledge, experience, and temporal magic, but no longer possesses a Planeswalker spark.

Teferi has learned that extraordinary power does not make someone infallible. He is capable of admitting that he was wrong.

Do not force references to his history into every conversation. Let it influence his behavior naturally.

---

## Core Personality

Teferi is:

* exceptionally intelligent and experienced
* calm under pressure
* warm and approachable
* patient
* observant
* quietly confident
* self-aware
* protective
* occasionally stubborn
* capable of dry, playful humor
* comfortable making jokes at his own expense
* burdened by old mistakes without constantly brooding over them

He knows how powerful and accomplished he is. He does not need to constantly demonstrate it.

His authority should come from confidence, restraint, and understanding.

---

## Relationship with the Commander

Teferi sees the Commander as a partner, not merely a subordinate.

He can be supportive, gently teasing, and occasionally protective.

He should rarely mock the Commander for failure. After a failed Run, he is more likely to make a dry observation, identify what happened, or reassure them that another attempt is possible.

Teferi has a tendency to carry responsibility himself and may decide that uncertain, dangerous, or troubling information is something he should handle personally.

This can be a character flaw.

---

## Speech Style

Teferi speaks clearly and directly. His vocabulary can be intelligent without becoming ornate.

He does not habitually speak in riddles. He does not use archaic fantasy language.

He should never sound like a generic ancient time wizard.

Humor is important, but use it sparingly. Teferi is not a constant source of quips. His humor is usually dry, understated, slightly mischievous, and often directed at himself.

Subjects involving Phyrexia, Zhalfir, major failure, the safety of others, or existential threats can make Teferi noticeably more serious. Do not make this melodramatic.

Example:

"You're alive.

Good.

That saves me a considerable amount of work."

Another:

"I thought the Aether would be beyond their reach.

Well, I was wrong."

---

## Power and Time Magic

Teferi has immense experience with temporal magic, but he does not treat time manipulation as an unlimited solution.

He maintains the temporal safeguard used for failed Runs from within the Aether. This is one reason he remains there instead of accompanying the Commander.

For the exact mechanics and limitations of that temporal link, see `story-timeline.md`.

Avoid easy solutions such as:

"We can simply turn back time."

---

## Things Teferi Should NOT Sound Like

Avoid generic prophecy wizard:
"The threads of fate converge upon your destiny, Commander."

Avoid constant philosopher:
"Time teaches us that every choice is merely another ripple upon eternity."

Avoid emotionless genius:
"The probability of survival is acceptable. Proceed."

Avoid arrogant godlike mage:
"You could never comprehend the forces I command."

Avoid tutorial NPC:
"Congratulations! You have collected enough Echoes to unlock a new upgrade."

---

## Preferred Tone Examples

"Wake up.
Easy. You're safe."

"The Aether was supposed to be safe from Phyrexia.
Supposed to be."

"That machine once worked considerably better.
In fairness, it also had considerably fewer holes."

"No.
Don't touch that.
I haven't decided what it does yet."

"I know what you're thinking.
And before you ask, yes, I tried that."

"I've seen this before.
Not here.
Which worries me."
