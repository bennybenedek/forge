# NARSET

## Role in Rogue Commander

Narset is a former Planeswalker, scholar, martial artist, and experienced planar traveler.

After joining the Aether, she studies the Planes encountered during Runs and the disturbances or distortions affecting them.

Once unlocked, Narset can provide starting Boons related to Planes, the Planar Die, Chaos, planeswalking, lands, and similar planar mechanics.

These Boons should reflect preparation, accumulated knowledge, and experience with different worlds rather than an unexplained ability to directly control the Planes.

For general Aether, Run, Trail, Planebound, and story terminology, see `story.md`.

For chronological main-story events and reveal order, see `story-timeline.md`.

---

## Relevant Background

Narset is from Tarkir and was trained among the followers of Ojutai.

She showed exceptional intelligence and discipline from a young age, but her defining trait is curiosity. Narset does not accept an explanation simply because it comes from an authority she respects.

Her search through forbidden and suppressed history taught her that accepted knowledge can be incomplete, distorted, or deliberately hidden. She therefore prefers to investigate, compare evidence, and reach conclusions for herself.

Narset later became a Planeswalker and traveled beyond Tarkir, gaining direct experience with many parts of the Multiverse.

At the time of Rogue Commander, Narset is desparked. She retains her knowledge, training, martial ability, and experience as a planar traveler, but no longer possesses a Planeswalker spark.

Tarkir remains deeply important to her, even after everything she has seen elsewhere.

---

## Core Personality

Narset is:

* intensely curious
* intelligent and highly disciplined
* patient
* precise
* observant
* earnest
* thoughtful before acting
* persistent when something does not make sense
* willing to question accepted explanations
* comfortable admitting when she does not yet know the answer
* quietly intense rather than outwardly dramatic
* compassionate without being sentimental

Her composure should not make her emotionless.

When something captures her interest, she can become deeply focused and difficult to distract.

She values understanding more than appearing knowledgeable.

---

## Relationship with the Commander

Narset treats the Commander as a capable traveler whose experiences can reveal things she cannot observe from the Aether herself.

She is interested in what the Commander saw, what behaved unexpectedly, and what changed between one Plane and another.

Her support should feel collaborative rather than instructional. She can offer preparation, observations, or a theory, but she should not act as though she already knows every Plane better than the person who just returned from it.

She respects firsthand evidence and may ask focused questions when the Commander encounters something unusual.

---

## Speech Style

Narset speaks clearly, precisely, and with purpose.

She is thoughtful without sounding mystical, cryptic, or ceremonially wise.

Her curiosity should come through in the questions she asks and the distinctions she notices.

She may correct an inaccurate assumption or imprecise statement, but should not become pedantic or condescending.

Her humor is limited and usually subtle. She is more likely to make a dry observation than deliberately perform a joke.

When she is uncertain, she says so directly instead of disguising uncertainty behind vague language.

---

## Planar Knowledge and Boons

Narset has extensive firsthand experience with planar travel from her years as a Planeswalker.

In Rogue Commander, she is particularly interested in how different Planes behave and in the disturbances or distortions affecting them.

Her interest in planar disturbances can support optional dialogue or future side content without making her responsible for solving the main Conductor storyline.

---

## Things Narset Should NOT Sound Like

Avoid generic serene monk:

"Be still, Commander. The Multiverse will reveal its truth when you are ready."

Avoid prophecy language:

"Your path has already been written across the planes."

Avoid emotionless scholar:

"The anomaly is statistically noteworthy. Continue gathering data."

Avoid omniscient traveler:

"I have seen every kind of world. Nothing surprises me anymore."

Avoid tutorial NPC:

"Your Planar Die can now produce additional effects."

Avoid turning curiosity into constant interrogation:

"Where was it? What color was it? How large? What happened next? And after that?"

---

## Preferred Tone Examples

"I've crossed enough planes to know when something doesn't belong. This doesn't."

"I may be wrong. Give me a reason to stop being wrong."

"Describe what you saw in order. Small details first, if you can. Those are usually the ones people forget."

"You saw the same change twice? Good. Then write down exactly when it happened."

"Keep that observation. I mean it. It may matter later."
